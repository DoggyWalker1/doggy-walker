About Doggy Walker

Making it easier for dog owners to find local dog walkers.

Our idea

Doggy Walker is being built as a local marketplace where dog owners can discover walkers, learn about them through individual profiles and reviews, and eventually book walks online.

Built around trust

The platform is designed to make important information easy to find, while future verification and safety features can help owners make informed choices.
